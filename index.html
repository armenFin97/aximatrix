Four phases. Fixed scope. No surprises.


We start fast on low-code and hand you the exit plan on day one — so you always know where you are, what comes next, and how your platform grows when you’re ready.

  • Phase-001 /

    Map

    We map your operation in detail — roles, workflows, data flows, pain points. No assumptions. We’ve worked across logistics, construction, healthcare and more. We know what to look for.

    Week 1 – 2

  • Phase-002 /

    Model

    Architecture, database design, role system, integration map. Your platform is designed before a single line of code is written. You see the blueprint. You approve it.

    Week 2 – 3

  • Phase-003 /

    Ship

    We build on Caspio for speed. Live in 6–12 weeks. Full platform — roles, automations, dashboards, documents, integrations. Weekly updates. No surprises on delivery day.

    Week 3 – 12

  • Phase-004 /

    When you’re ready to scale beyond low-code — your architecture moves cleanly to React / Vue + AWS. No vendor lock-in. No forced rebuild. The system grows with you.
